# Your Cozy Science Lab guide

Open **[The Cozy Science Lab](https://julomjohnlouise19-cloud.github.io/SHEN-LOVE-EMU-HOUJO/)** and refresh once after an update. Keep your browser's site data so device practice and notebook entries stay saved.

1. **Join your classroom.** Choose Create account, use a Gmail inbox you can open, and enter the latest **eight-digit verification code** from that inbox. Existing students use their usual login; no separate friend username is needed. Guest exploration is also available.
2. **Make the lab yours.** Open your profile → Appearance. Select **Meadow**, **Aurora** or **Sunset**; the whole interface changes and saves automatically. Wait for the saved message. Turn **Dark mode** on or off in the same area. Dark mode is saved for your account on this device. Use **Save my style** when your introduction text is ready. In the same Appearance panel, use **Sound on / Sound off** and choose **Animation style → Keep it calm** to reduce motion. Your device’s accessibility preference is always respected. The quiz also has a direct mute button.
3. **Add your pictures and badges.** Profile photos save after a successful upload. Use **Edit banner**, choose an image, adjust the focus, then **Preview & finish → Apply my banner**. JPG, PNG and WebP are good choices. The preview lets you check phone and wide layouts before saving. In **Appearance → Your little badge shelf**, tap up to three earned badges, then **Save my badges**. They appear on your profile above the settings. Remove or clear pins whenever you like, then save. **Undo changes** restores the saved shelf. Pins stay with your learning account on this device.
4. **Start a discovery.** Open Science Island, choose a branch/topic and difficulty, or enter Discovery Activities for matching, Word Hunt, flashcards and Planet Explorer. Read feedback after an answer and revisit tricky ideas in Review Box. **Study Room → Science videos** has **50 distinct videos, ten in each of five science categories**: search a topic, press **Watch here**, and use the related notes and supporting source. **Original video / Open on YouTube** opens the creator's page if the player is unavailable. The other shelves keep notes, sources, models, PDF handbooks and the periodic table.
5. **Grow your scientist.** Open **My growth → Cozy buddies** to meet eleven new animals. **Your next little win** shows up to four suggested milestones/buddies; open any eligible badge → **Make this my little goal** to choose another without a long deck list. Use its practice action or Review Box shortcut to work toward the task. Your XP grows through **Curious Seedling (Level 1)**, **Lab Assistant (5)**, **Junior Scientist (10)** and **Professor (20)**; expand **Your cozy title trail** to explore them. The collection has 43 badges: eleven Cozy buddies, five milestones and 27 topic explorers. Use the collection/search/Earned only filters or **Arrange my badge shelf** to display favorites. The [Cozy crew guide](COZY_BADGES.md) lists every new name and task. Learning XP/badges stay with this account on this device.
6. **Connect with classmates.** Science friends opens to classmates and recent activity. Use a student's existing login username to send a request; they accept it to become friends. The leaderboard orders current **class-credit balances highest first**. Spending credits can change your position.
7. **Claim daily rewards.** Use Daily spin for one free class-credit spin per classroom day. Your result and next-spin date appear together and update automatically; a zero-credit spin leaves your balance unchanged. The bell reminds you if a spin is available. The gift button opens the **49-day / seven-week** claim calendar; claim the current dated day and browse weeks to see progress. Missed days cannot be backfilled. Shared rewards require your verified student account and the classroom's enabled reward service. Practice merits are a separate balance.
8. **Need a hand?** New students first see **Customize my profile**, with cute shortcuts to their photo/name, banner preview and colors/Dark mode. Press **? beside the title logo** for the website introduction and six interactive tour stops. **Show me the step-by-step guide** reopens the welcome/profile tips. Choose **Don’t show again** when you have learned the ropes; your account remembers that choice on this device. You can still reopen the guide manually. **X** closes a panel and returns you to your page. About us introduces the student researchers; Suggestion box sends feedback to your teachers.

**My lab** is a short welcome with one suggested discovery or saved-session action, three small progress details and **Vocabulary of the day**. Reveal the word's meaning, read its notes/sources or practise its topic. The word changes at classroom midnight. The small **Your little week** calendar lets you tap a date for its real answers and correct connections, browse earlier weeks or return to this week. Its quiet **My growth** link opens the badge garden. Use the menu for the full categories and tools.

**Science Island → Explore activities** opens a four-island discovery map: **Connection Cove** for Match Lab, **Wordwood Grove** for Word Hunt, **Memory Meadow** for Flashcards and **Starlight Lookout** for Planet Explorer. Choose a science branch, enter an island or use **Continue round**. The map link remembers the branch after a refresh. The passport dock opens your earned activity badges. See the [discovery map guide](DISCOVERY_MAP.md) for the controls and saved-progress details.

Open the **calendar icon beside Your little week in My lab** for your full Snoopy discovery diary. My growth stays focused on titles, badges and your next learning goal. Tap a date to see its answers, correct connections and eight-answer progress. **Choose month** opens your saved history; **Earlier practice / Later practice** jump between days with answers, including across months. **Show today** returns to the current classroom day. Eight answers add a cute daily stamp. Meadow, Aurora and Sunset give the diary different colors, frames and companions. New activity from another tab appears automatically. These device practice records are separate from the shared daily-reward claim calendar.

**Study Room → Illustrations → Try the tiny lab** opens the interactive physics experiment.

**Study, breathe, then quiz:** Press **Focus & practise** in Study Room or inside any guide, handbook, video, model or periodic table. Handbooks have readable chapter buttons plus optional PDF preview, open and download actions. Choose 5, 15, 25 or 50 minutes and **Start focus**. The timer stays in the **lower-right corner**, shrinking into a small clock with pause and X controls. Tap the clock to expand it. It keeps the same remaining time as you switch study materials; press a resource's Focus & practise button to use that topic for your quiz. **Pause timer / Resume timer** keeps the remaining time, and **Finish studying** moves on early. Then take an optional five-minute break (ten for a 50-minute focus), or choose **Quiz: [topic]**, select a difficulty and start. A general Study Room/handbook session instead offers **Choose a topic quiz**. X ends the timer; closing a resource keeps it running. Reloading or logging out ends it. Only actual quiz answers earn learning progress. Flowers, stars and hearts follow your saved palette and light/Dark mode.

Newly earned badges give a small notification. Open the **bell → Achievements** to revisit saved badge/level updates. In **Profile → Appearance**, turn learning sounds on, set **Sound volume**, choose **Sound to preview** and press **Preview selected sound**. There are familiar cues for correct answers, gentle retries, hints, card flips, completions, badges and level-ups, with clearer sustained tones and softer transitions. Correct answers get a brief themed confetti burst, including Discovery Activities; it works while sound is muted. Choose **Keep it calm** to turn particles off; your device’s reduced-motion setting also turns them off. Reopening saved feedback does not replay a celebration. Sounds start off; the quiz also has a mute control. Volume is saved for this account on this device, and reduced motion remains available.

Forgot your password? Choose **Sign in → Forgot your password?**, enter your account email, and press **Send reset link**. The resend button shows its cooldown. Open the latest reset link in the same browser, enter matching new passwords and save. **Sign in instead** is a separate action beneath the form. Delivery errors do not claim that mail was sent.

Teachers can open **Teacher studio** to manage students, learning records, supplies, suggestions and classroom settings. The existing reward calendar, credit rankings and class-credit wheel are installed in this classroom. Study Room videos, My lab vocabulary, the focus timer, learning calendar, achievement/sound improvements and logos need **no database update or YouTube API key**. Cached notes/catalogs work offline; YouTube playback and external publisher pages need internet.

For the site owner: keep **Confirm email** enabled and **Email OTP length = 8**. The website validates the full code with the account service. The [email setup guide](EMAIL_CODE_SETUP.md) includes the updated eight-digit email wording/template if you need to update the private sender template. Keep its `{{ .Token }}` placeholder unchanged.
