# Your Cozy Science Lab guide

Open **[The Cozy Science Lab](https://julomjohnlouise19-cloud.github.io/SHEN-LOVE-EMU-HOUJO/)** and refresh once after an update. Keep your browser's site data so device practice and notebook entries stay saved.

1. **Join your classroom.** Choose Create account, use a Gmail inbox you can open, and enter the latest **eight-digit verification code** from that inbox. Existing students use their usual login; no separate friend username is needed. Guest exploration is also available.
2. **Make the lab yours.** Open your profile → Appearance. Select **Meadow**, **Aurora** or **Sunset**; the whole interface changes and saves automatically. Wait for the saved message. Turn **Dark mode** on or off in the same area. Dark mode is saved for your account on this device. Use **Save my style** when your introduction text is ready. In the same Appearance panel, use **Sound on / Sound off** and choose **Animation style → Keep it calm** to reduce motion. Your device’s accessibility preference is always respected. The quiz also has a direct mute button.
3. **Add your pictures.** Profile photos save after a successful upload. Use **Edit banner**, choose an image, adjust the focus, then **Preview & finish → Apply my banner**. JPG, PNG and WebP are good choices. The preview lets you check phone and wide layouts before saving.
4. **Start a discovery.** Open Science Island, choose a branch/topic and difficulty, or enter Discovery Activities for matching, Word Hunt, flashcards and Planet Explorer. Read feedback after an answer and revisit tricky ideas in Review Box. **Study Room → Science videos** has 23 videos in five science categories: search a topic, press **Watch here**, and use the related notes and supporting source. **Original video / Open on YouTube** opens the creator's page if the player is unavailable. The other shelves keep notes, sources, models, PDF handbooks and the periodic table.
5. **Grow your scientist.** Open **My growth** or use the shortcut in your profile. Your existing practice XP grows through **Curious Seedling (Level 1)**, **Lab Assistant (5)**, **Junior Scientist (10)** and **Professor (20)**. The badge garden has five milestones and one explorer badge for each of the 27 science topics, using your science-animal characters. Tap a badge for its criteria and a study shortcut; use the topic, search and Earned only filters. A perfect easy badge needs all eight answers correct in one easy deck. Learning XP/badges stay with this account on this device.
6. **Connect with classmates.** Science friends opens to classmates and recent activity. Use a student's existing login username to send a request; they accept it to become friends. The leaderboard orders current **class-credit balances highest first**. Spending credits can change your position.
7. **Claim daily rewards.** Use Daily spin for one free class-credit spin per classroom day. Your result and next-spin date appear together and update automatically; a zero-credit spin leaves your balance unchanged. The bell reminds you if a spin is available. The gift button opens the **49-day / seven-week** claim calendar; claim the current dated day and browse weeks to see progress. Missed days cannot be backfilled. Shared rewards require your verified student account and the classroom's enabled reward service. Practice merits are a separate balance.
8. **Need a hand?** New students first see **Customize my profile**, with cute shortcuts to their photo/name, banner preview and colors/Dark mode. Press **? beside the title logo** for the website introduction and six interactive tour stops. **Show me the step-by-step guide** reopens the welcome/profile tips. Choose **Don’t show again** when you have learned the ropes; your account remembers that choice on this device. You can still reopen the guide manually. **X** closes a panel and returns you to your page. About us introduces the student researchers; Suggestion box sends feedback to your teachers.

On **My lab**, reveal the **Vocabulary of the day** and open its notes/sources or practice topic. The word changes at classroom midnight. **My learning calendar** and **My growth → Learning calendar** show actual dated quiz-answer totals; tap days, browse earlier practice months or use **Show today**. These device practice records are separate from the shared daily-reward claim calendar.

Newly earned badges give a small notification. Open the **bell → Achievements** to revisit saved badge/level updates and follow their growth-garden links. In **Profile → Appearance**, turn learning sounds on and use **Preview a soft sound**; the quiz also has a mute control. Sounds start off, and reduced motion remains available.

Teachers can open **Teacher studio** to manage students, learning records, supplies, suggestions and classroom settings. The existing reward calendar, credit rankings and class-credit wheel are installed in this classroom. The Study Room videos, vocabulary, learning calendar, achievement/sound improvements and new logos need **no database update or YouTube API key**. Cached notes/catalogs work offline; YouTube playback and external publisher pages need internet.

For the site owner: keep **Confirm email** enabled and **Email OTP length = 8**. The website validates the full code with the account service. The [email setup guide](EMAIL_CODE_SETUP.md) includes the updated eight-digit email wording/template if you need to update the private sender template. Keep its `{{ .Token }}` placeholder unchanged.
