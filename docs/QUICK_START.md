# Your Cozy Science Lab guide

Open **[The Cozy Science Lab](https://julomjohnlouise19-cloud.github.io/SHEN-LOVE-EMU-HOUJO/)** and refresh once after an update. Keep your browser's site data so device practice and notebook entries stay saved.

1. **Join your classroom.** Choose Create account, use a Gmail inbox you can open, and enter the latest **eight-digit verification code** from that inbox. Existing students use their usual login; no separate friend username is needed. Guest exploration is also available.
2. **Make the lab yours.** Open your profile → Appearance. Select **Meadow**, **Aurora** or **Sunset**; the whole interface changes and saves automatically. Wait for the saved message. Turn **Dark mode** on or off in the same area. Dark mode is saved for your account on this device. Use **Save my style** when your introduction text is ready.
3. **Add your pictures.** Profile photos save after a successful upload. Use **Edit banner**, choose an image, adjust the focus, then **Preview & finish → Apply my banner**. JPG, PNG and WebP are good choices. The preview lets you check phone and wide layouts before saving.
4. **Start a discovery.** Open Science Island, choose a branch/topic and difficulty, or enter Discovery Activities for matching, Word Hunt, flashcards and Planet Explorer. Read feedback after an answer and revisit tricky ideas in Review Box. Science reading room has notes, sources, diagrams, handbooks and the periodic table; My notebook keeps your own thoughts on this device.
5. **Connect with classmates.** Science friends opens to classmates and recent activity. Use a student's existing login username to send a request; they accept it to become friends. The leaderboard orders current **class-credit balances highest first**. Spending credits can change your position.
6. **Claim daily rewards.** Use Daily spin for one free class-credit spin per classroom day. The bell reminds you if it is available. The gift button opens the **49-day / seven-week** claim calendar; claim the current dated day and browse weeks to see progress. Missed days cannot be backfilled. Shared rewards require your verified student account and the classroom's enabled reward service. Practice merits are a separate balance.
7. **Need a hand?** Press **? beside the title logo** for the cute introduction and six interactive tour stops. Use Previous/Next, tap a stop, or use arrow/Home/End keys. **X** closes it and returns you to your page. About us introduces the student researchers; Suggestion box sends feedback to your teachers.

Teachers can open **Teacher studio** to manage students, learning records, supplies, suggestions and classroom settings. The existing calendar, credit rankings and class-credit wheel are installed in this classroom; no new database step is required for color styles or the guide.

For the site owner: keep **Confirm email** enabled and **Email OTP length = 8**. The website validates the full code with the account service. The [email setup guide](EMAIL_CODE_SETUP.md) includes the updated eight-digit email wording/template if you need to update the private sender template. Keep its `{{ .Token }}` placeholder unchanged.
