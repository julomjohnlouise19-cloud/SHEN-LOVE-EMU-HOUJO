# Meet your Cozy crew

Open **My growth → Cozy buddies** to meet the eleven new animals. Each buddy has a learning task, visible progress and a useful practice shortcut. Tap a badge to read its full task.

| Buddy | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| Brave Little Bean | Give 20 science answers. Useful mix-ups count too. |
| Study Snuggle | Practice on three different classroom days; they do not need to be consecutive. |
| Brainwave Wizard | Finish an eight-question medium deck with at least six correct. |
| Pocket Medic | Complete Cells and Anatomy & Body Systems. |
| Connection Cat | Complete a deck in three different science branches. |
| Clipboard Captain | Finish three eight-question decks with at least six correct in each. Repeat practice is welcome. |
| Careful Lab Paws | Finish an eight-question Science Skills Lab deck with at least seven correct. |
| Aha Again | Revisit five different Review Box questions correctly. |
| Fizz & Purr | Complete two different Chemistry topics. |
| Notebook Navigator | Complete five different science topics. |
| Big-Idea Paws | Finish an eight-question hard deck with at least six correct. |

The Skills Lab opens at the learning level shown in the app. Until then, **Build my Skills Lab level** opens a gentle practice deck. Review Box questions appear when an answer needs another look; review has its own buddy task.

**Your next little win** offers up to four suggestions from milestones and Cozy buddies. To choose another, open a badge and press **Make this my little goal**. Topic explorers remain in **Science topics**; the goal chooser stays short.

To display a buddy, open an earned badge → **Display on my profile**, or choose **Arrange my badge shelf**. Tap up to three earned badges and press **Save my badges**. Use **Undo changes** to restore your saved shelf, or remove pins and save again.

There are **43 learning badges**: eleven Cozy buddies, five milestones and 27 topic explorers. Existing achievements and profile pins stay with your progress. Learning badges and pins are saved for your account on this device. Practice earns the usual XP and merits; badges are keepsakes, while class credits have their own classroom wallet.
