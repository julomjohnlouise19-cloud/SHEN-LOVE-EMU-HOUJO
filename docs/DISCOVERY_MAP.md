# Your little discovery map

Open **Science Island → Explore activities**. Choose your science branch, then pick a place on the map.

| Place | Activity | Your little adventure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connection Cove | Match Lab | Match six science terms with their meanings. Tap a pair or use a mouse to drag. |
| Wordwood Grove | Word Hunt | Follow six hidden words through the letter garden, then connect them with their meanings. |
| Memory Meadow | Flashcards | Recall a term, flip its card, then choose **I know this** or **Practice again**. |
| Starlight Lookout | Planet Explorer | Read the field notes and answer each planet check to collect eight stamps. |

Your science branch stays in the map link after a refresh. **Continue round** opens your saved round; the little status beside each island shows real progress. If a saved round belongs to another branch, the game explains the choice before replacing it.

Use **How to play** for a short guide, **A little hint** for a nudge and **Learning sources** to read the ideas behind a round. Sound is optional and starts off. Completed activities earn keepsakes in **Your discovery passport → Open my badge collection**.

The map follows Meadow, Aurora or Sunset and your light/Dark setting. On phones it becomes a vertical trail with large destinations. Keyboard users can Tab to a destination and press Enter; reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Rounds and activity badges stay with this account on this device. Browsing the map does not award XP or class credits. The artwork and activities work offline after the site has been cached.
